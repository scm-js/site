Replays

A replay opens as an analysis: build orders, fights and graphs on a timeline, with the game itself playing beside them. From any moment you can take a side and play on.

Drop a replay .rep From Brood War 1.16 or StarCraft: Remastered. It is read in this browser and nothing is uploaded. The build orders and the timeline need no game files; the game's own picture needs your StarCraft.
