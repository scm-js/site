scmJS in numbers

What people do in the editor at scmjs.dev, added up: the tilesets and sizes they pick, the units they place, how often they undo, and when they map.

These come from people who left counting on in Preferences ▸ General ▸ Privacy. The desktop app and copies hosted elsewhere send nothing. A value is left out unless at least three different people sent it on one day, and it was sent on ten person-days in all. Nothing from today is shown, and every number is rounded. The privacy page says exactly what is counted.
