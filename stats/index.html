How These come from people who left counting on in Preferences ▸ General ▸ Privacy. The desktop app and copies hosted elsewhere send nothing. A value is shown only once three different people have sent it on the same day, and ten in all. Nothing links one day's visits to the next, so someone who comes back on another day counts again. Nothing from today is shown, and every number is rounded. The privacy page says exactly what is counted.
